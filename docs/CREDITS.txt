Made with these tools (the results belong to the game)
------------------------------------------------------
- Surfaces, signs and portraits: Google's Gemini image model (Vertex AI), tools/make_textures.py and
  tools/make_portraits.py.
- Film pictures: FLUX.2 [dev] by Black Forest Labs (run locally); their motion: LTX-2.3 by Lightricks (run
  locally); encoded as AV1.
- Music: ACE-Step 1.5 (run locally, the soundtrack mode of the AudioGenerator), tools/music.json.
- The places' sound beds: Stable Audio Open 1.0 by Stability AI (run locally), tools/ambience.txt.
- Voices: Qwen3-TTS by the Qwen team, Alibaba Cloud (Apache 2.0, run locally), tools/make_voices.py.
- Sound effects: synthesised by the game itself.
