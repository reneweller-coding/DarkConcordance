# Level 4: The Drowned Quarter (English). Lines, hints, terminals.

l4.name        = The Drowned Quarter
l4.intro       = The Second Drum has turned more slowly since its bearings failed. In one of its quarters the tanks overflowed; without weight, water gathers into balls, and the balls stayed. | Whoever could not swim stayed as well. | Through the quarter runs the Skimmers' way to the Core Bank. Its pumps and locks are kept by a man they call the Pumpwright.
