l7.intro       = Hesper is the gas giant below Seren. In its atmosphere the Concession extracts deuterium and helium-3 for its fusion drives, and the refinery floats on balloons of hot hydrogen – the only thing that floats in a hydrogen atmosphere. | Balloons as big as city quarters, below them a thousand kilometres of storm. Lightning that stands between the cloud bands for minutes. | Each balloon is held aloft by a control core. The cores from Seren went here.
