# Level 23: Inside the Mill (English).

l23.name  = Inside the Mill
l23.intro = A hollowed moon three hundred kilometres across, turning slowly for gravity in its outer layers. In the hollow centre hangs a fusion-heated smelting furnace, a small artificial sun; around it, molten metal flows along the walls. | There are hardly any people. Thousands of control cores run the Mill. The few human overseers live in a comfortable residential block and never set foot in the halls. | Some cores deliberately shoot wide. Others fight grimly, because every mistake earns them penalty time.
