# Control hints any level can show (English).
# "<key>.dorra": how it applies to Dorra; "-" means nothing for her.

h_fire          = Shoot: right trigger or left mouse button. The rifle takes the nearest target ahead of you.
h_fire.dorra    = Strike: right trigger or left mouse button. Hold it for the whole chain, up to the overhead blow.
h_strafe        = Left trigger / Shift holds your facing: back off and keep firing.
h_strafe.dorra  = Left trigger / Shift: shield up. It stops shots and blows from the front.
h_bolt          = -
h_bolt.dorra    = B / F: throw a blast bolt – for whatever the hammer can't reach.
h_stim          = Q / Y: inject a stim (+40 health).
h_heat          = The rifle has overheated. Ease off for a moment.
h_heat.dorra    = -
