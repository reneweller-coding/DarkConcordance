# The creatures: one family each, its three grades below.
codex.foe.crystal = Grown inside the object of Tesh, not built. They stand where they grew and fire splinters of light at whatever comes close. Bullets and blows half glance off; shock makes them shatter.
codex.foe.shade = Something that lives between the moments in the ark and the object of Tesh. A phase shade is suddenly beside you, strikes, and is gone again. Bullets half pass through it; it does not bear heat well.
codex.foe.eel = Cable eels hunt in the water of the Drowned Quarter: they lunge out of the dark and bite. Current does nothing to them – they live on it. Acid eats them.
codex.foe.diver = Diving drones of the old pumping works, switched to defence. They keep their distance and shoot; shock cripples their electronics.
codex.foe.beast = Bred in the Dying Gardens for the arena. A beast takes a run-up, charges in a straight line and tears down whatever stands in its way. Dodge, don't stand. Acid goes through its hide.
codex.foe.vine = Vines of the Dying Gardens, rooted: close by they lash, further off they spit. Fire is their end; acid they know from the soil.
codex.foe.frost = Cores of frozen gas drifting in the cold, on the Computing Moon and the Wanderer. They keep their distance and shoot cold. Heat melts them.
codex.foe.spider = Ice spiders leap from the rime of drilling rigs, holds and computing moons. Quick and light – heat makes them slow, then still.
codex.foe.golem = What rises from the Mill's slag. Heavy, slow, unstoppable once it gets going. Fire rather feeds it; acid breaks it down.
codex.foe.magma = Crawlers from the glowing rock of Brannt and the Mill. They leap out of the embers. Heat does nothing to them; shock scares them off.
codex.foe.phantom = In the Net and the Control Room: images from the cores' memories, fighting back. They flicker, appear beside you and shoot from the side. Shock tears them apart.
codex.foe.glitch = Faults in the Net, small and quick, in swarms. Break one and you have two. Blows and shock hit them well, acid hardly.
codex.foe.crab = Crabs of the crust, on Halden and on Brannt. Their armour is in front: attack from the side. They charge. Acid dissolves the armour.

# The foes.
codex.foe.louse = Rust lice: maintenance drones of the Cooling Guts, six legs in a tripod gait, long gone feral. They crouch and leap. One alone is nothing; twenty are a problem.
codex.foe.welder = Welder lice keep their distance and spit molten metal in high arcs. Where it lands, the floor glows for a while.
codex.foe.baton = Skimmers: the people who take the control cores out of the uninsured's heads. A baton, red-black plates, no conscience.
codex.foe.gunner = Skimmers with carbines. Their bolts fly slowly enough to dodge – if you see them coming.
codex.foe.shocker = Skimmers with shock rifles: a hit stuns for a moment. Take them out first.
codex.foe.shield = Shield guards carry a riot shield and a hammer. From the front they stop nearly everything; from the side or behind they are people like any other.
codex.foe.heavy = Heavy skimmers: broader, taller, a rotary gun. When it spins up, find cover.
codex.foe.drone = Guard drones hover above the floor and fire from above. Fragile, but rarely alone.
codex.foe.saw = Saw bots: small dark lice with a blade on their backs. They come low and fast.
codex.foe.bomber = Fuses: striped yellow and black, with a charge that glows. They run into you. Their blast hits the others too.
codex.foe.taxator = Brass and black, twice a man's height, an arm cannon. The Taxator assesses what you are worth, then leaps.
codex.foe.shell = Shells: printed bodies Mother Sevenfold wears like clothes. While the link holds they can hardly be hurt.
codex.foe.flamer = Clearance squads with flamethrowers. Short reach, a lot of fire. Keep your distance.
codex.foe.sniper = Snipers: their laser rests on you for a while before they fire. Step out of the line.
codex.foe.balloon = Guards on balloons above the clouds of Hesper. Hit the balloon and the guard comes down.
codex.foe.cutter = Torch cutters: fast, tough, their torch goes through any plate. Don't let them close.
codex.foe.turret = Turrets guard corridors and gates. They turn slowly – come from the side.
codex.foe.hound = Clearance hounds: big low runners in dark plates. They hunt in packs.
codex.foe.pumpdrone = Pump drones of the Drowned Quarter, rebuilt as guards. They keep the water and you at bay.
codex.foe.swarmling = The Collegium's drones, small alone, together a cloud.
codex.foe.hive = Swarm carriers build new swarm drones. First the carriers, then the swarm.
codex.foe.mender = Maintenance robots of the Computing Moon: they repair what you break. First them, then the rest.

# The bosses.
codex.boss.press = The drone press in the Cooling Guts' drone works: a ram on an anvil, hatches dropping new lice. Two coolant tanks are its weak points; behind them lies the core.
codex.boss.taxator = The Taxator of the Marrow Market guards its hall. It assesses, it shoots, it leaps; and it calls whoever helps it.
codex.boss.tug = The tug "Grace Period" on the Outer Skin: a gun and two engines, shells with red rings, drones. Get out of the rings.
codex.boss.hydraulic = The Hydraulic of the Drowned Quarter: broad, oxidised green-blue, a great wrench in his fist.
codex.boss.mother = Mother Sevenfold in the Core Bank: seven bodies and three radio masts linking them. First the masts, then the bodies.
codex.boss.rig = The Icebreaker on Seren drills through the ice above the ocean. What it has to guard sits in its core.
codex.boss.bell = The Bell above the clouds of Hesper. It rings, and the balloons come.
codex.boss.okafor = Clearance Chief Okafor on Halden, in her walker: olive, broad, a rifle arm. She can be talked to – sometimes.
codex.boss.leader = The boarding leader in the ark, in an exoskeleton with a cutting torch for a fist. He stamps, fires, calls his cutters and charges at the end.
codex.boss.salvager = The Salvage Master in the object of Tesh, in a frame of hazard stripes. He wants what grew there.
codex.boss.lance = The Plasma Lance of the star lifter of Kael: a weapon that thinks, or a core that has to be a weapon.
codex.boss.lamm = Teodor Lamm, champion of the Dying Gardens, in his crimson arena frame. He dies twice – the arena prints him anew.
codex.boss.chief = The cleaners' chief in the Yard at the Gate, in the Collegium's grey. His people delete evidence; he deletes whoever finds it.
codex.boss.swarm.drones = The Collegium's drones at the hearing: a cloud that orders itself and attacks.
codex.boss.swarm.menders = The defence of the Computing Moon: swarm and maintenance robots healing each other.
codex.boss.swarm.archon = "No cause for concern": so it introduces itself, in Vault Zero. An archon of the Collegium, friendly to the last.
codex.boss.platform = The cutting platform above Velm: lasers cutting lines into the floor. Step out of the line before it fires.
codex.boss.column = The cutting column peels Brannt. One of its cutters hesitated – and talks afterwards.
codex.boss.catcher = The Catcher at the end of the Ore Stream: a huge funnel, arms plucking containers out of the stream. Its clock runs; every part destroyed buys time.
codex.boss.foreman = The Mill's foreman in his crane walker, bigger than all the others. He can be spared.
codex.boss.varn = Ysolde Varn in her violet frame, with a blade. She paid your bill.
codex.boss.swarm.delete = The deletion processes in the Net: they take what the cores are, piece by piece.
codex.boss.dreier = Hakon Dreier, from Corvin's tale. A man Corvin would rather not remember.
codex.boss.siel = Major Siel, from Dorra's tale. He gave the orders; she dug.
codex.boss.brask = Shift Supervisor Brask, from Ione's tale. He wanted the units reset. Reset means delete.
codex.boss.editor = "Thorough Review", from Unzu's tale: the review he was not meant to pass.
