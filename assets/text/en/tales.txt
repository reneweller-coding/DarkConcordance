# Corvin: a confession
tale.corvin.name  = A confession
tale.corvin.intro = The mess of the "Insufficiently Equipped", late. Unzu has found something he calls schnapps and dimmed the lights without anyone asking. | Corvin turns his glass until the light in it stands still. "I was a skimmer. You know that. What you don't know is how good I was at it." | Nine years ago. Odile, a moon in the Fringe where it has rained since the day it was settled.
tc_a1        = It was raining on Odile. It always rains on Odile. Hakon Dreier said rain was good for business: people stay at home, and the houses have numbers.
tc_a2        = You were one of them.
tc_a3        = I was the best of them, Dorra. That's the part I usually leave out.
tc_b1        = The settlement was called Kessel. Four hundred people, none of them backed up. Dreier had a list. We worked down it, house by house, door by door.
tc_b2        = The list. Who wrote it?
tc_b3        = Someone with a desk, Ione. It's always the ones with the desk.
tc_c1        = The device isn't loud. A hum, a light, then the head is empty, and the body goes on breathing for a while. You get used to it. That's the worst thing I can say about myself.
tc_c2        = Kessel, Odile system. I surveyed the settlement. Four hundred and two inhabitants, fifty-eight of them children. The number is probably wrong by now.
tc_d1        = The last house had a child's drawing on the door. A yellow boat. Dreier said: the little one first. Children fetch more, they have longer to live.
tc_d2        = I put my device down on the floor. Then I shot at Dreier. And missed. I had never missed before.
tc_d3        = Good.
tc_champion  = Gerrit. Five years we worked together. He didn't hesitate for a second. Neither did I.
tc_hurt      = I bled like anyone else. That surprised me back then.
tc_boss      = Aske! You were my best. What's this – a conscience? A conscience costs extra, and you can't afford it.
tc_boss2     = Dreier sat in the lifting rig he loaded the bodies with. He called it "the crane". We called it that too. Nobody called it what it was.
tc_end       = Dreier lay in the rain. He was still laughing when I took the list out of his pocket. Then it was quiet. And behind a bin, something yellow was shaking.

I interrupted myself back then too." | Three years ago. The star lifter Kael, maintenance deck three, night shift.

# Dorra: a war chronicle
tale.dorra.name  = Day forty-one
tale.dorra.intro = Ysolde's flat on the ring, after the shipyard. Outside, the land climbs into the sky. Dorra lays her forearms on the table, insides up. There is nothing there any more. She looks anyway. | "A chronicle. I'll tell it the way we kept it back then: date, situation, losses. No heroes. We didn't have any." | Twenty years ago. Kalvar, a mining world of almost twice Earth's gravity, in revolt against its incorporation.
td_a1        = Kalvar, day forty-one of the uprising. There were three hundred of us left. The Concordance's clearance squads held the valley, we held the tunnels. Gravity was on our side. Nothing else was.
td_a2        = Forty-one days. How long did it last, the uprising?
td_a3        = Forty-three, Ione.
td_ally      = We built the barricade guns out of drill heads. They didn't hit well. But they hit.
td_b1        = Josse Rhee led the left tunnel. Josse sang when he was afraid. On day forty-one he sang all day.
td_b2        = (sings) "… and down in the mountain it's dark and it's warm, and up there is no one who asks …"
td_c1        = The conveyor station Deep Seven. The ore ran through it to the Concordance's ships. If it stopped, Kalvar stopped. That was the plan.
td_c2        = And the people in the station?
td_c3        = They said it had been evacuated, Corvin. They said a lot of things.
td_d1        = Josse fell at the station's entrance. He wasn't singing any more. I set the charges alone. Twelve of them, as we'd practised.
td_d2        = Deep Seven isn't in my data any more. A crater, filled in. I never asked why.
td_champion  = A squad leader with a flamethrower. I knew him. Before the war he was a teacher in Deep Three.
td_boss      = Vask! Surrender. Kalvar will be incorporated, with you or without you. People will be better off afterwards. That isn't a threat. It's a forecast.
td_boss2     = Major Siel. She came herself, in a walker. That set her apart from the others.
td_end       = Vask … there are still people in the station. The night shift. Forty-one. We couldn't get them out.
td_end2      = Siel was still alive. She told me there were still people in the station. I didn't believe her. I didn't want to believe her.
td_fuse      = The detonator lay where Josse had put it down.
z1           = (The detonator is live. In the windows of Deep Seven the lights are still on. Behind you, engines in the valley: the next column.)
z1_a         = (detonate) For Kalvar.
z1_b         = (wait)
z2           = (You press. For one breath nothing happens.)
z3           = (You wait. Two minutes. The lights in the station don't go out. The engines come closer.)
z3_a         = (detonate)
tale.dorra.end = Forty-one. I learnt every name.

# Unzu: a survey log
tale.unzu.name  = Survey log 3.112
tale.unzu.intro = The mess, on the way to Velm. Unzu has had his memories back since the computing moon and has said less than ever since. Tonight he dims the lights by himself. | "I'll read you my log, entries 3.112 to 3.115. Don't interrupt, or I'll lose my place. That was a joke. I never lose my place. Only the entries." | Thirteen years ago. The Fringe, the Halde-Three system, the settlement of Talgrund. Unzu's survey drone is flying. The memory of it is being erased right now – then as today.
tu_a1        = Survey log, entry 3.112. Fringe, Halde-Three system, settlement of Talgrund. Task: record the population by suitability for resettlement.
tu_a2        = Talgrund. The sixty-one in the container.
tu_a3        = Back then there were four hundred and twelve, Dorra. I'm still counting.
tu_count     = Recorded. Everyone gets a number. I told them it doesn't hurt. That was true. Back then.
tu_b1        = Entry 3.113. The inhabitants are friendly. They offer the drone tea. A child asks whether I can count.
tu_b2        = Can you count? How far? I can count to a thousand, nearly.
tu_b3        = I said: that's all I can do. It didn't believe me. It was right.
tu_c1        = Entry 3.114. The category "fit for resettlement" requires a forecast of adaptation to the Concordance. I cannot make one. I enter: not assessable.
tu_c2        = You refused.
tu_c3        = I left a field empty, Ione. For an archon that's the same thing.
tu_d1        = Entry 3.115. … This entry has been revised. Entry 3.115: The population of Talgrund is fit for resettlement. Signed: Insufficiently Equipped. – I did not sign that.
tu_d2        = Who did, then?
tu_d3        = Someone who sounded like me, Corvin. They had taken my voice before they took the rest.
tu_census    = Everyone counted that I could find. At least that stays in my log. At least the number.
tu_boss      = Unit, your entries contain errors. We are correcting them now. It does not hurt. You will not remember that it hurt.
tu_end       = The correction was aborted. – No. That isn't true. Back then it wasn't aborted, back then it worked. Only today I tell it differently.
tale.unzu.end = Talgrund: four hundred and twelve inhabitants. Not assessable.
